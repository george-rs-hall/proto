Solution · The check

Jev Asks Two Questions.

Does it fit?

Does the cart match what you asked for and what you’ve told us before?

Is it a trick?

Is the merchant trying to talk the agent into it?

Solution · Undo

Easy To Undo? Less Need To Be Sure.

expected loss =
(1 − P(fits)) × cost to undo

The merchant signs its undo window and cancel endpoint. Free returns mean nothing’s at risk, so the wallet just pays.

Solution · Four outcomes

No Approve Button. No Surprises.

PAY

Fits, and cheap to undo.

ASK

Fits, but costly if wrong.

DECLINE

Doesn’t fit, or an injection attempt.

BLOCK

Merchant tried injection before.
